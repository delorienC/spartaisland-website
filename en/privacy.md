## 2. Core principle: your data stays on your device

Sparta Island is deliberately designed as a **local app**. All data you enter — training sessions, personal records, body data, nutrition diary, training plans — is stored **exclusively on your device** (SQLite database).

- There is **no registration and no user account**.
- **No data is transmitted to the provider.**
- The app contains **no tracking, no analytics services, and no crash reporting**.
- The app works **fully offline**.

## 3. The single exception: nutrition lookup via Open Food Facts

When you actively use the **barcode scanner** in the nutrition feature, the app fetches nutrition facts for the scanned product from the **Open Food Facts** database (`world.openfoodfacts.org`).

- **Data transmitted:** the scanned barcode, your device's IP address (a technical necessity of any internet request), and an app identifier (user agent).
- **When:** only at the moment you scan a barcode — never in the background.
- **Purpose:** retrieving nutrition facts (calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat) for the scanned product.
- **Recipient:** Open Food Facts, a non-profit organization based in France (EU). Open Food Facts privacy information: <https://world.openfoodfacts.org/privacy>
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR — the transmission is necessary to provide the feature you requested (nutrition lookup).

The retrieved nutrition facts are then stored locally on your device. If you do not use the barcode scanner, no data is transmitted at all.

## 4. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20), and objection (Art. 21), as well as the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR).

Since all app data resides exclusively on your device and the provider stores no personal data about you, you exercise control over your data directly: deleting the app irrevocably removes all data.
